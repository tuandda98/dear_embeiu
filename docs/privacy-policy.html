Privacy Policy — dear embeiu

Last updated: 8 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application dear embeiu (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in plain text).
  • Couple information: partner names, anniversary date, optional couple photo, and an invite code used to pair two accounts.
  • Photos and captions: images you upload to the in-app gallery and any captions you add. These are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage and visible only to you and your paired partner.
  • Device information: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token, OS platform (iOS/Android), and notification preference, used to deliver push notifications to your partner’s device.
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs and basic app activity collected by Firebase services to help us improve reliability.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To pair your account with your partner via the invite-code flow.
  • To sync your shared couple data (profile, gallery) between paired devices in real time.
  • To deliver a push notification to your partner when you post a new photo.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is shared only:

  • With your paired partner inside the App (couple profile, gallery, captions, name shown on each post).
  • With our infrastructure provider Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions), which processes data on our behalf under Google’s privacy and security commitments.

4. Data storage and retention

  • Account, couple, photo, and device data are stored in Firebase services (Google Cloud) and protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Some data is also cached locally on your device for offline access.
  • We retain your data while your account exists. You can request deletion at any time (see section 6).

5. Push notifications

The App requests permission to send push notifications. Notifications are sent only when your paired partner posts a new photo. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings or in-app preferences.

6. Your rights — access and deletion

You can:

  • View and edit your account and couple profile inside the App.
  • Delete photos you have uploaded.
  • Leave a couple, which removes you from the shared space.
  • Request full account and data deletion by emailing us at thesavior9820@gmail.com. We will process the request within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Security

We rely on Firebase security rules and TLS encryption in transit. While no system is 100% secure, we follow industry-standard practices to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at thesavior9820@gmail.com.
